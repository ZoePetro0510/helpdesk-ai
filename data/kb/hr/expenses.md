# Expenses

## Submitting an expense
Submit expenses in the Expenses app within 60 days of the purchase. Take a photo of the receipt and choose the category. Your manager approves the expense and it is paid with the next salary.

## What can be expensed
Business travel, client meals, approved courses from your learning budget, the home office allowance and work equipment that IT could not supply can be expensed. Personal meals during normal working days cannot.

## Travel
Book flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is used for flights under 6 hours. The daily allowance while travelling abroad is $60 for meals.

## Lost receipts
If you lost a receipt, submit the expense with a short written explanation. Lost-receipt expenses above $100 need approval from the finance team.
