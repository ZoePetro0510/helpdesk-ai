# Benefits

## Health insurance
All employees are covered by the company's private health insurance from their first day. Partners and children can be added in the HR portal within 30 days of joining or of a life event such as marriage or birth.

## Pension
The company contributes 6.5% of your salary to your pension fund and you contribute 6%. You can choose or change your pension fund once a year in the HR portal.

## Learning budget
Each employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, conferences and books related to their role. Ask your manager for approval, then submit the receipt as an expense.

## Wellness allowance
Employees receive a $50 monthly wellness allowance for gym memberships, sports classes or similar. It is paid automatically with the salary.

## Meal card
Employees receive a meal card with $15 per working day, loaded on the first day of each month.
