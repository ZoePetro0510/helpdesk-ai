# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Hybrid policy
Employees work from the office at least 2 days per week. Each team chooses its shared office days, usually Monday and Wednesday. The other days can be worked from home.

## Home office equipment
Employees can order a monitor, keyboard and mouse for home through the IT catalog. A one-time home office allowance of $300 covers a desk or chair; submit the receipt as an expense within 60 days of joining.

## Working from abroad
You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per year with your manager's approval. Longer periods need HR approval because of tax and legal rules. Notify IT before travelling so your access is not blocked.

## Working hours
Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00, when everyone should be reachable. Outside core hours you can arrange your time with your team.
