# Sick Leave

## Reporting sick
If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day and record the sick day in the HR portal. You do not need to share medical details with your manager.

## Medical certificate
For an absence of 3 or more consecutive working days, upload a medical certificate to the HR portal within 5 days of returning to work.

## Sick days and pay
Employees receive 12 paid sick days per year. Sick days are paid at 100% of salary from the first day.

## Sick child or family member
Up to 8 of your sick days per year can be used to care for a sick child, partner or parent. Record them in the HR portal as "family sick day".
