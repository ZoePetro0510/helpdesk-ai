# Payroll

## Pay date
Salaries are paid on the 9th of each month for the previous month. When the 9th falls on a weekend or public holiday, salaries are paid on the last working day before it.

## Payslips
Payslips are published in the HR portal under "My documents" by the 7th of each month. Past payslips stay available for 7 years.

## Changing your bank account
Update your bank details in the HR portal under "Personal details". Changes made before the 25th apply to the next salary. For security, HR confirms every bank change by phone.

## Mistakes in your salary
If you think your salary is wrong, open a helpdesk request for the HR payroll team with the month and what you expected. Payroll corrections are paid with the next salary, or within 5 working days for errors above $500.

## Tax forms
Annual tax forms are published in the HR portal by January 31.
