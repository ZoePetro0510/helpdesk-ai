# Vacation and Leave

## Vacation days
Full-time employees receive 18 vacation days per year, increasing to 22 days after 3 years. Part-time employees receive days in proportion to their hours. The vacation year runs from January 1 to December 31.

## Requesting vacation
Request vacation in the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance for absences of 3 days or more. Your manager approves the request in the portal. Shorter absences need at least 2 working days' notice.

## Carrying over unused days
Up to 5 unused vacation days can be carried over to the next year and must be used by March 31. Days above 5 are lost unless your manager and HR approve an exception in writing.

## Checking your balance
Your current vacation balance is shown on the home page of the HR portal and on your monthly payslip.

## Parental leave
Birth parents receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Other parents receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave, which can be taken within the first year. Notify HR at least 8 weeks before the planned start date.
