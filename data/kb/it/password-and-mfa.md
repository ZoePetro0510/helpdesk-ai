# Passwords and Multi-Factor Authentication

## Resetting your password
You can reset your own password at any time from the self-service portal (portal.orbit.internal/reset). You will need your company email and a code from your authenticator app. Passwords must be at least 14 characters and cannot reuse any of your last 5 passwords. The new password works on all company systems within 10 minutes.

## Locked account
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account locks for 30 minutes and then unlocks automatically. If you need access sooner, contact the IT helpdesk and an agent will verify your identity by video call before unlocking it.

## Setting up MFA on a new phone
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for every employee. When you change phones, open the self-service portal from your laptop, choose "Security info", remove the old device and scan the QR code with the authenticator app on the new phone. Keep the old phone until the new one is working.

## Lost access to your MFA device
If you lost the phone with your authenticator app and cannot sign in, IT must issue a temporary access code. This requires identity verification by video call with your manager present, so open a helpdesk request and mark it urgent if you cannot work.

## Password managers
The company-approved password manager is Bitwarden Teams. Every employee receives a license on their first day. Storing work passwords in browsers or personal notes is not allowed.
