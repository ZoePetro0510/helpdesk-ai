# Hardware Requests

## Standard equipment
Every employee receives a laptop (MacBook Pro or Dell Latitude, depending on role), a 27-inch monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset. Developers may choose a second monitor.

## Requesting new equipment
Request accessories such as monitors, docking stations, keyboards or headsets through the IT catalog in the self-service portal. Items under $150 are approved automatically and shipped within 5 working days. More expensive items need your manager's approval in the portal.

## Laptop replacement
Laptops are replaced every 3 years. If your laptop is broken, very slow or has a damaged screen before then, open a helpdesk request with a description and photos. IT will repair it or provide a loaner laptop within 2 working days.

## Damaged or lost equipment
Report damaged or lost equipment to IT within 24 hours. A lost or stolen laptop must also be reported as a security incident so IT can lock and wipe it remotely.

## Returning equipment
When you leave the company or receive a replacement, return the old device to the IT desk or use the prepaid return box that IT sends to your home.
