# Security Incidents

## What counts as a security incident
A lost or stolen device, a suspicious email you clicked, a password you shared by mistake, unknown software on your laptop, or customer data sent to the wrong person are all security incidents.

## Reporting a phishing email
Do not click links or open attachments. Use the "Report phishing" button in Outlook, which sends the email to the security team and removes it from your inbox. If you already clicked a link or entered your password, report it as an urgent security incident immediately and change your password.

## Reporting an incident
Report any security incident at once through a helpdesk request marked urgent, or call the security hotline at extension 9111 outside working hours. Speed matters more than having all the details.

## After you report
The security team contacts you within 1 hour during working hours. Do not try to fix the problem yourself and do not delete evidence such as emails or files.
