# VPN and Network Access

## Connecting to the VPN
Use the GlobalProtect client, which is pre-installed on every company laptop. Enter the portal address vpn.orbit.internal, sign in with your company email and approve the MFA prompt. The VPN is required for internal systems such as the finance system, the HR portal and code repositories.

## VPN keeps disconnecting
First check that your laptop is on the latest company update and restart it. If the VPN still drops more than once an hour, switch from Wi-Fi to a wired connection or a phone hotspot to rule out your home network. If the problem continues, open a helpdesk request with the time of each disconnect.

## Office Wi-Fi
Employees connect to the "Orbit-Staff" network with their company credentials. Guests use the "Orbit-Guest" network; the daily password is shown on the screen at reception. Personal devices may only use the guest network.

## Working from abroad
The VPN works from most countries. Before working from abroad for more than 5 working days, notify IT at least one week in advance so your account is not blocked by the location-based security policy.
